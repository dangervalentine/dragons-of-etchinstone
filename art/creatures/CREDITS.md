# Creature and landscape art

Every silhouette here was generated for this project with FLUX.1-dev from the subject
lines in `art/STYLE_PROMPT.md`: the four dragons, the sixteen enemies (after the
creatures on the official cards, then finished by hand) and the seventeen journey
scenes (first drafts, to be reworked). The enemies and journeys were framed with
`tools/frame_art.py`, and all were converted with `tools/vectorize_art.py`; their
sources are in `art/source/`.
