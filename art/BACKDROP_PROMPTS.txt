ENEMY BACKDROP PROMPTS (backgrounds for the Enemy panels)
==========================================================

One background per Enemy, drawn behind its painted figure on its Region Card panel
and on the Encounter face (ui/widgets/region_panel.gd, encounter_face.gd). Built as
the dragon lairs are (LAIR_PROMPTS.txt, its lessons and its round-2 GROUND STYLE):
each prompt is SUBJECT + REGION + ETHER + BACKDROP STYLE, written out in full under
READY TO PASTE.

Settings: FLUX.1-dev, text to image, 1216 x 896 (FLUX's native size nearest a panel's
shape, about 1.38 : 1), no LoRA, no Redux, guidance 3.5, euler, 25 steps, 4 seeds
each.

What a panel needs from the picture:
- The figure stands with its feet on the panel's foot, centred in the right 83%
  (just right of the middle), so the floor under it is level and empty.
- The badges (HP, Initiative, Attack, Armor) run down the left 17%, on dark chips;
  that side is kept plain.
- Soft middle tones, as Helsang's round 2 found: a dark ground made a pale figure
  look cut out; the figure keeps its own ink outline and backlight.

No prompt names a creature, a person, a monk or a fighter: any of them gets drawn.


BACKDROP STYLE (appended to every prompt)
-----------------------------------------
A flat vector illustration of an empty landscape seen from the side, made as the
background of a game card: minimalistic graphic design in flat colour, made of big,
simple shapes with smooth, clean edges. Every form is one flat, matte colour, with at
most a single hard-edged darker shade tone. A limited, muted palette of soft middle
tones, close together and low in contrast, with the Ether's colour as the only bright
one. A wide composition seen from a low eye level: the lower quarter of the picture
is a broad, flat, level open floor, plain and empty from edge to edge, and the
horizon sits about a third of the way up. Above it is one wide, calm, plain area,
lightest just right of the middle and darkening slowly toward the edges. The scene's
landmarks stand behind the floor at the far right edge and small in the distance; the
left fifth of the picture is plain and calm. The place is empty and uninhabited,
silent and ancient.

REGION
------
  1  It lies in the Jade Mountains, a land of tall, narrow, sheer-sided karst pillars of green-grey rock, twisted pines and drifting banks of mist.
  2  It lies in the Living Marshes, a land of still black water, tall reeds, weeping willows and gnarled trees standing on arching stilt roots, under low drifting mist.
  3  It lies in the Burning Basin, a land of dark ash slopes, black basalt and cracked lava crust, rust-red sand and dark glassy rock, under a deep, smoky dusk sky of rust and charcoal, lightest only in a low haze, with a far volcano glowing. Every tone is deep and dusky; nothing in it is pale.
  4  It lies in the Forest of Gath, a land of huge, ancient, gnarled trees with thick roots, deep green shade, moss, old stone stairs and rope bridges over gorges.

ETHER (follows every subject, from the Enemy's element)
  water      Icy blue light glows from [PLACE], with curling wisps of frost and mist.
  shadow     Violet light glows from [PLACE], with curling wisps of smoke.
  fire       Ember-red firelight glows from [PLACE].
  lightning  Amber-orange sparks and arcs crackle over [PLACE].


R1.1 SHAOLIN PANDA  (panda-fighter · lightning)
-----------------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A mountain monastery's training yard: a flat courtyard of worn grey flagstones, a
curved temple roof and a carved wooden gate at the far right edge, and tall karst
pillars rising in the mist far behind.

ETHER:
Amber-orange sparks and arcs crackle over the distant peaks, under a low, dark storm
cloud.


R1.2 LIONEL ARCHER  (lion-archer · fire)
----------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A high mountain terrace at dusk: a flat ledge of packed earth and stone, a low stone
wall with a round straw archery target at the far right edge, a few twisted pines,
and karst pillars in the evening haze far behind.

ETHER:
Ember-red firelight glows from a brazier on the wall and along the edges of the low
evening clouds.


R1.3 CRANE MONK  (crane-monk · water)
-------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
The still shore of a mountain lake at the foot of a waterfall: a flat shore of smooth
pale stone, a tall waterfall pouring down a karst cliff at the far right edge into
the lake, a few reeds, and misty karst pillars standing in the water far behind.

ETHER:
Icy blue light glows from the falling water, with curling wisps of frost and mist
drifting low over the lake.


R1.4 FRENZIED APE  (monkey-brawler · shadow)
--------------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A dark mountain forest before a cave: a flat clearing of packed earth, thick bamboo
stems and twisted pines at the far right edge, a rocky cliff with a cave mouth behind
them, and karst pillars in the mist far behind.

ETHER:
Violet light glows from deep inside the cave mouth, with curling wisps of smoke
drifting out of it.


R2.1 SWAMP TREANT  (marsh-treant · shadow)
------------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A drowned forest in a still marsh: a flat, level bank of firm, mossy peat in front,
gnarled swamp trees standing on arching stilt roots in still dark water at the far
right edge, long curtains of hanging moss, and pale willows in the mist far behind.

ETHER:
Violet light glows from a hollow in the largest tree, with curling wisps of smoke
drifting low over the water.


R2.2 RAPID GNOME  (axe-gnome · lightning)
-----------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A woodcutter's clearing at the edge of the marsh: a flat, level patch of dry, packed
earth, a broad tree stump with a few split logs and a stack of firewood at the far
right edge, and reeds and willows in the mist behind.

ETHER:
Amber-orange sparks and arcs crackle over the far willows, under a low, dark storm
cloud.


R2.3 BOG OGRE  (club-ogre · fire)
---------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A bog at dusk: a flat, level causeway of trodden mud and rough wooden planks across
it, a crude hut of reeds and mud raised on stilts at the far right edge, and still
dark pools and reeds behind.

ETHER:
Ember-red firelight glows from a cooking fire in front of the hut, lighting the mist
from below.


R2.4 GOBLIN RAIDER  (horned-goblin · water)
-------------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A raiders' camp on a river bank: a flat, level bank of grey sand and pebbles, an
upturned wooden boat and a few crude spears stuck in the ground at the far right
edge, and the slow black river and leaning willows behind.

ETHER:
Icy blue light glows from the river, with curling wisps of frost and mist drifting
low over it.

Region 3, round 2: round 1's pale ash, crust and sand washed out the white Ability
disc and left the figures little to stand against. Deep, dusky tones throughout.

R3.1 CONJOINED GHOUL  (twin-ghoul · shadow)
-------------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A field of old graves on an ash slope: a flat, level ground of dark grey ash, a few
leaning grave markers and a broken stone coffin at the far right edge, and charred
dead trees and the far volcano in the haze behind.

ETHER:
Violet light glows from inside the broken coffin, with curling wisps of smoke rising
from it.


R3.2 TWIN OGRE  (twin-ogre · water)
-----------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A hot spring in the volcanic basin: a flat, level shelf of dark basalt, steaming
pools of water in terraces of dark stone at the far right edge, and grey ash slopes
behind.

ETHER:
Icy blue light glows from the pools, with curling wisps of frost and mist rising off
them.


R3.3 NAGA GORO  (naga · fire)
-----------------------------
SUBJECT:
A ruined desert temple at the edge of the lava fields: a flat, level floor of dark
red sandstone flagstones swept with rust-red sand, broken red sandstone columns and a
carved stone gateway at the far right edge, and glassy dunes and the smoking volcano
behind.

ETHER:
Ember-red firelight glows from a slow river of lava behind the columns.


R3.4 WEREBOAR  (boar-shaman · lightning)
----------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A ritual ground in the glass desert: a flat, level circle of packed rust-red sand, a
ring of tall, jagged pillars of dark glass and stone hung with strips of hide at the
far right edge, and dunes and flat-topped mesas behind.

ETHER:
Amber-orange sparks and arcs crackle between the tops of the glass pillars.


R4.1 GOBLIN HEXER  (goblin-hexer · shadow)
------------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A hollow in the deep forest: a flat, level clearing of dark packed earth, a huge
hollow tree hung with bundles of herbs and small bone charms at the far right edge,
ringed by a few toadstools, and great trunks in the gloom behind.

ETHER:
Violet light glows from inside the hollow tree, with curling wisps of smoke drifting
out of it.


R4.2 HEDGE WIZARD  (hedge-wizard · fire)
----------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A cottage at the forest's edge: a flat, level path of packed earth, a crooked cottage
with a thatched roof behind a tall dark hedge at the far right edge, and ancient
trees behind.

ETHER:
Ember-red firelight glows from the cottage window and from a lantern hung by its
gate.


R4.3 ANTLERED HORROR  (antler-horror · lightning)
-------------------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
A misty glade in the old forest: a flat, level floor of short moss, a ring of tall,
weathered standing stones at the far right edge, and two huge gnarled trees leaning
together into an arch behind.

ETHER:
Amber-orange sparks and arcs crackle between the standing stones, under a low, dark
storm cloud.


R4.4 BRUTE OF GATH  (gath-brute · water)
----------------------------------------
SUBJECT:
The edge of a forest gorge: a flat, level ledge of mossy stone, the end of a sagging
rope bridge tied between two great trees at the far right edge, and a waterfall
falling into the gorge behind.

ETHER:
Icy blue light glows from the waterfall, with curling wisps of frost and mist rising
from the gorge.
